# Charge dispute window

## Deadline
Northwind customers may dispute a card charge within 60 days of the statement date that shows the charge.

## How to start
Open a dispute in the Northwind app under Transactions, or call Northwind support with the merchant name, amount, and date.

## What happens next
Northwind reviews the claim and may issue a temporary credit while the investigation is open. Final decisions are sent by email or in-app message.
