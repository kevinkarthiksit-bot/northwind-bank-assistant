# Savings interest

## How interest is calculated
Northwind Savings interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited monthly, on the date shown in the product terms.

## Where to find the rate
The current savings interest rate is published on the Northwind rates page and inside the app under Accounts, Savings. Chat must not invent a rate.

## Tax
Interest may be subject to tax as required by local rules. The statement shows interest credited; tax details are in the annual interest summary.
