# Statement charges

## Monthly account fee
Northwind Current accounts show a monthly account fee on the statement when the average monthly balance is below the published minimum. The fee amount is listed on the Northwind fee schedule page, not invented in chat.

## ATM fees
Northwind ATM withdrawals at Northwind machines are free. Other-bank ATM withdrawals may show a network fee on the statement.

## How to read a charge
Each statement line shows the date, merchant or fee name, and amount. Pending authorizations can appear before they post as final charges.
