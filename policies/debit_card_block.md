# Debit card block

## When to block
Block a Northwind debit card as soon as the customer reports it lost, stolen, or used without permission.

## How to block
The customer can block the card in the Northwind mobile app under Cards, or by calling Northwind support. After a block, the card cannot be used for purchases or ATM withdrawals.

## Replacement
A replacement card is mailed to the address on file within 5 business days. Temporary card numbers are not issued by chat.
