# KYC document list

## Identity
Northwind accepts a government photo ID for identity verification: passport, driver's licence, or national ID card.

## Address
Proof of address must be dated within the last 3 months. Accepted examples: utility bill, bank statement from another bank, or government letter.

## How to submit
Upload documents in the Northwind app under Profile, Verify identity. Do not send photographs of documents over public chat.
